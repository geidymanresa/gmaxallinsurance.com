1.What cookies are

Cookies are small text files that a website saves in your browser or device when you visit it. They are widely used so that websites work properly, remember your preferences, and to understand how they are used.

2.Types of cookies we use

3.Third-party cookies

We may use third-party tools (for example, web analytics) that set their own cookies when you visit the Site. These tools have their own privacy policies, which we recommend you review. We do not directly control the cookies these third parties may set.

4.How to control or disable cookies

You can accept or reject non-essential cookies from the cookie notice that appears when you visit the Site. You can also manage or delete cookies directly from your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Keep in mind that blocking all cookies (including essential ones) may prevent some parts of the Site, such as the quote form, from working correctly.

5."Do Not Track" signals and Global Privacy Control

Some browsers send a "Do Not Track" signal or a Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal. When we detect a valid GPC signal from a Texas resident, we will treat it as a valid request to opt out of the sale of data or targeted advertising, as Texas law requires — although, as explained in our Privacy Policy, we do not currently engage in those activities.

6.Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time, for example if we add new analytics or advertising tools. The "Last updated" date shows the current version.
