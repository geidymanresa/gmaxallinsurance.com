SMS Opt-In and Program Details

Last updated: November 10, 2026
Summary: GMAX All Insurance sends text messages only to people who requested an insurance quote or bought a policy and agreed to receive texts by checking an optional, unchecked box on our quote form. Consent is never required to get a quote or buy a policy. Questions? Call (832) 480-0217 or email info@gmaxallinsurance.com.

1.Program

Program name: GMAX All Insurance customer text messages.

Who sends the messages: Manresa Multiservices LLC, doing business as (d/b/a) GMAX All Insurance, an independent insurance agency in Houston, Texas. GMAX All Insurance is the trade name used by Manresa Multiservices LLC.

Types of messages: follow-up on an insurance quote you requested, payment reminders, policy renewal reminders, and replies to questions you send us by text.

Message frequency: up to 8 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply.

2.How you opt in

You opt in with the optional SMS consent checkbox on our public quote form: https://gmaxallinsurance.com/cotizar.html. The checkbox is unchecked by default, it is separate from the required Terms checkbox, and you can submit the form and receive your quote without checking it. Opting in is not a condition of purchase or of receiving any service.

Exact consent language shown next to the checkbox (English):

Yes, I want to receive text messages from GMAX All Insurance (follow-up on my quote and reminders about my policy and payments) at the mobile number I entered above. Up to 8 messages per month. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel or HELP for help. Consent is optional and is not a condition of purchase or of receiving services. We do not share your mobile number with third parties for marketing purposes. See our Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy.

Spanish translation shown below it:

Sí, quiero recibir mensajes de texto de GMAX All Insurance (seguimiento de mi cotización y recordatorios de mi póliza y pagos) al número que escribí arriba. Hasta 8 mensajes al mes. Pueden aplicar tarifas de mensajes y datos. Responde STOP para cancelar o AYUDA para ayuda. Es opcional y no es condición para comprar ni para recibir servicios. No compartimos tu número de celular con terceros para fines de mercadeo.

For reference, the required checkbox above it on the form is about quotes by phone and email, not text messages. Its English translation: “I have read and accept the Terms and Conditions and the Privacy Policy of GMAX All Insurance, and I authorize a licensed agent to contact me by phone call or email for insurance quoting purposes. I understand I can call (832) 480-0217 directly instead.”

We record each consent with the date and time, the phone number, the exact text shown and the page where it was given.

3.Opting out and getting help

  • Opt out: reply STOP at any time (CANCEL, QUIT, END, UNSUBSCRIBE, STOPALL, OPTOUT and REVOKE also work). We send one confirmation message and then no more messages. Reply START to subscribe again.
  • Help: reply HELP, call (832) 480-0217, or email info@gmaxallinsurance.com.

4.Privacy

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell or share your phone number or your SMS opt-in data. See our Privacy Policy and the SMS program terms in section 8 of our Terms and Conditions.
