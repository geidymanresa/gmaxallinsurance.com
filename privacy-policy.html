Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026
Important: This document is provided for general information. GMAX All Insurance is an independent insurance agency located in Houston, Texas. Questions? Call (832) 480-0217 or email info@gmaxallinsurance.com.

1.Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how GMAX All Insurance ("GMAX," "we," "us") collects, uses, shares and protects information obtained through gmaxallinsurance.com, our agents' public pages, our quote tools, and our communications with you as a customer or prospective customer (together, the "Services").

2.Information we collect

CategoryExamples
Identity and contact dataName, address, phone number, email address

We collect this information directly from you when you complete a quote form, contact an agent, or use the Services, and automatically through cookies.

3.How we use your information

  • To prepare quotes and manage your insurance request.
  • To communicate with you about your quote, policy, renewal or payments, including by phone, text message and email, based on your consent (see our Terms and Conditions).
  • To meet our legal and regulatory obligations as a licensed insurance agency in Texas.
  • To improve our website and services, and for internal analytics.
  • To prevent fraud and protect the security of our systems and users.
  • For our own marketing (for example, telling you about other products that may interest you), always subject to your right to opt out.

4.Who we share your information with

  • Insurance carriers: we share the information needed to prepare quotes, issue policies and process claims.
  • Service providers: companies that help us operate the website, process payments, send communications (email and text) or host our data, under confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal requirements: when required by law, a court order or a regulator such as the Texas Department of Insurance.
  • Corporate transactions: if GMAX is merged, acquired or sells assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

We do not share your information with third parties so they can do their own direct marketing without your consent.

Text messaging (SMS) privacy: No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or phone number with third parties. Information is shared only with the messaging provider that delivers our messages, strictly so that they can be sent. You can cancel text messages at any time by replying STOP. Program details (frequency, rates and how to opt out) are in section 8 of our Terms and Conditions.

5.Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies so the website works properly and to understand how it is used. Essential cookies are needed for the site to function. Optional cookies, if any, are used only for analytics and improving our services. You can control cookies in your browser settings, and you can reset your cookie choice on our site at any time. We also respect Global Privacy Control signals.

6.We do not sell your information for third-party advertising

GMAX does not sell your personal information to third parties for their own targeted advertising, and we do not use sensitive data or children's data for targeted advertising. If this changes in the future, we will update this policy and give you the option to opt out, as Texas law requires.

7.Your rights under Texas law (TDPSA)

If you are a Texas resident, the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act gives you the right to:

  • Confirm and access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your personal information, subject to legal exceptions (for example, records we must keep as an insurance agency).
  • Obtain a portable copy of your information in a usable format.
  • Opt out of the processing of your information for targeted advertising, the sale of personal data, or automated decisions with significant legal effect. We do not currently engage in these activities, but we honor this right.
  • Appeal if we deny a privacy rights request.

To exercise these rights, contact us as described in section 13. We respond within 45 days of your request (with a possible 45-day extension, which we will notify you about). We do not charge for up to two requests per calendar year. We will verify your identity before processing your request.

8.Security

We maintain reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, use or disclosure. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of information sent over the internet.

9.Data retention

We keep your information as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy, including as long as Texas insurance laws and regulations require, which typically means keeping customer records for several years after the relationship ends.

10.Children's privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

11.Residents of other states

Although we focus on Texas, we may serve residents of other states where we are authorized to operate. If your state gives you additional or different privacy rights, we will honor them as required by applicable law.

12.Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date shows the current version. We will communicate important changes in a reasonable way, such as a notice on our website.

13.Contact and exercising your rights

  • Email: info@gmaxallinsurance.com
  • Phone: (832) 480-0217
  • Location: Houston, Texas

If you are not satisfied with our response to a privacy rights request, you may file a complaint with the Texas Attorney General.

14.Social media: Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp

When you comment on our posts or message us on Instagram, Facebook or WhatsApp, we receive your name or username, your platform ID, the text of your comment or message, and its date and time. We do not receive your password or access to your personal account.

How we use it: to reply to you, send the information you requested, follow up about insurance and, if you give us your phone number or email, prepare a quote. Some replies may be automated when you send a keyword or message, and you can always ask to speak with a person on our team.

Who it is shared with: information travels through Meta platforms (Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp), which have their own privacy policies, and is stored in our internal customer management system, which only authorized agency staff can access. We do not sell this information or share it with third parties for their own marketing.

How to stop messages: reply STOP and we will stop writing to you.

How to delete your social media data: email info@gmaxallinsurance.com with the subject "Delete my data" and your Instagram or Facebook username, or send us the word DELETE in a direct message. We will delete the information we received from that network, except what the law requires us to keep, within the timeframes in section 7.
